What's inside

One app, every safeguard an exam needs.

Designed with Regis Marie College's Computer Studies faculty to close the gaps that make classroom mobile exams easy to cheat and hard to trust.

Screenshot prevention

Disables the device's screen‑capture function while an exam is active, so content can't be saved or shared.

App exit restriction

Keeps students inside the exam — the app can't be minimized or switched away from while a test is running.

Copy‑paste blocking

Turns off copy and paste inside the exam interface, so questions and answers can't move in or out of the app.

Question randomization

Shuffles the order of exam questions differently for every student, so no two seats see the same sequence.

Role‑based access

Students see only their enrolled classes; teachers manage exams and records; admins oversee the whole system.

Automated grading & reports

Objective‑type answers — multiple choice, true/false, identification, enumeration — are scored instantly, with class and institution‑wide reports for teachers and admins.

How it works

From check‑in to submitted grade.

01

Log in to your class

Sign in with your Regis Marie College account to see the exams you're enrolled to take.

02

Screenshots, app‑switching, and exiting are disabled the moment the exam starts.

04

Submit and get graded

Objective‑type answers are scored the moment you submit, and your result is added to your record.

Security isn't a setting here. It's the whole design.

Three restrictive security features run by default on every exam, with no facial recognition, video, or audio monitoring involved — just controls on the device itself.

Screenshot prevention

Screen capture is disabled the whole time an exam is active.

App exit restriction

Students can't leave or minimize the app while a test is in progress.

Copy‑paste blocking

Copy and paste are turned off inside the exam interface.

Question randomization

Each student gets their own question order, cutting down on peer‑to‑peer copying.
